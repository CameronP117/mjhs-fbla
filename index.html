About

What FBLA actually is

A quick rundown before you decide whether to join.

FBLA is a national student organization for people interested in business and leadership careers, with chapters in nearly every state, including ours, right here at Maynard Jackson. Members go to conferences, compete in events tied to real business skills, and meet other students who care about the same things they do. You don't need to already know Excel formulas or have a business plan ready; most members join without knowing what "supply chain management" even means, and figure it out along the way.

Why students actually join

Find What You're Good At

With dozens of competitive events in design, public speaking, coding, and more, there's usually one that matches something you already like.

You Get Real Leadership Experience

Running a fundraiser or leading a project for a competition gives you leadership experience that's hard to get elsewhere in high school.

You build a Network early

Conferences put you in a room with students and business professionals from across the state, connections outlasting highschool alone

About · College Applications

How FBLA actually helps your application

Admissions officers read a lot of "member of a club" lines. Here's what makes FBLA different.

A specific story, not a vague one

A state or national qualifying score, an officer title, or a fundraiser you led gives you a real answer to "tell me about a time you led something" because you'll actually have one.

Scholarship access

FBLA members are eligible for FBLA scholarships and, in some cases, scholarships from partner universities that specifically look for FBLA membership.

A backed-up interest

Saying you're interested in business, finance, or marketing on an application means more when there's something you actually did behind it.

Early exposure to real skills

Public speaking, interviewing, and presenting under pressure at conferences are skills that carry straight into college interviews and beyond.

About · Scholarships

FBLA scholarships

On top of the leadership experience, FBLA membership opens the door to real scholarship money.

What's available

National FBLA offers a range of scholarships to members — some tied to competitive event performance, others open more broadly to members pursuing business, finance, marketing, or related fields in college. Award amounts, eligibility rules, and deadlines are set by national FBLA and can change year to year, so the official page is always the source of truth.

View scholarships on FBLA.org

How to be eligible

You generally need to be a current, dues-paid FBLA member to apply. Some scholarships also require competing in specific events or reaching certain conference levels — check each listing's requirements on the national site.

Questions?

Ask your chapter advisor or an officer — they can help you figure out which scholarships you qualify for and walk you through the application process.
